What it does

A whole assistant. Not another chat window.

Hands-free

Say “Clover”, ask, and keep talking. It reads answers aloud and chimes when it’s your turn. Say “copy that”, “say that again” or “thanks Clover”.

Dictate anywhere

Hold fn, talk, let go. Your words land in any text field, cleaned up, with the “um”s gone. Or word for word, on your Mac.

Sees your screen

Ask about anything you’re looking at. Ask “where do I click?” and it points at the spot.

Draws to explain

“Explain this and draw it” builds a labelled diagram right on your screen, step by step.

Gestures

Built to be touched.

Clover answers your trackpad the way Apple’s own apps do: things follow your fingers, keep their speed when you let go, and can be caught again mid-flight.

Flick it away

Two fingers sideways. Past a third of the way, or flicked, it flies off and closes.

Pinch it small

Tucks into a little bar with its live steps while Clover keeps working.

Pull it back

Pull the bar down with two fingers, or pinch out, to open it again.

Force click to hear

Press harder on an answer. It sinks in with your finger and reads aloud.

Swipe a card down

Agent cards and ideas go with two fingers down: Later, or ✕.

Turn the page

Swipe between sections in Settings and steps in setup. The ends stretch and bounce back.

Momentum, not distance

A flick is judged by where it would land, so a quick short throw works as well as a long drag.

Catch it mid-air

Put your fingers back while the box is springing and it stops right under them. Nothing jumps.

Feel the click

On a Force Touch trackpad, a light tick when a gesture passes the point of no return, and one when it lands.

Rubber-band edges

Where there is nowhere to go, things stretch a little and come back instead of stopping dead.

Scrolling stays scrolling

Only clearly sideways movement counts, and never over a table or code block that scrolls that way.

Calm when you need it

With Reduce Motion on, every spring and flight becomes a quick fade.

Private by default

Yours, on your Mac.

Clover sends nothing anywhere except to the AI you picked. No account, no telemetry, nothing to sign up for.

Your voice stays here

Apple’s on-device speech turns your words into text. Audio never leaves your Mac, and the wake word is recognized locally.

You choose how much it asks

Ask first before anything changes, Auto that only asks before deleting or buying, or Bypass. Every command is in the ask log.

Hard limits, always

In every mode, for every provider, rm, sudo, disk tools, shutdown and git push stay blocked.
